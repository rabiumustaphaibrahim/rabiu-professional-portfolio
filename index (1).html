About

From power systems to the energy behind industry.

I graduated with a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from Kano University of Science & Technology, Wudil, and I am now pursuing an MSc in Sustainable Energy Systems at the Centre for Renewable Energy & Sustainability Transitions (CREST), Bayero University Kano.

My interests have moved from electrical engineering and power systems toward renewable energy, energy efficiency, productive-use energy, circular economy and technology-driven development.

Time spent around industrial sites, infrastructure projects and plastic recycling has made one thing clear to me: industries that recover and reuse resources need energy that is reliable, affordable and clean. That question, how sustainable energy can support circular industries in Nigeria and across Africa, is the direction I am building toward, one step at a time.
